# Onboarding — porting notes

## Ported (logic only)
- A checklist per joiner in phases (before joining, joining day, first week, first month); ticks keep who and when,
  unticks keep the row. Progress over live items.
- "Already joined" ticks the before-joining and joining-day items.
- Convert to employee: needs code and joining date; makes the employee master record or links one with the same code
  and the same person; never takes over someone else's code.
- Joiners and items are switched off with a reason, never deleted. HR roles only, within their branches.

## Became settings / organisation data
- The checklist is HR's own (Onboarding → Checklist items). A neutral starter list is installed; nothing names a
  company, its systems or its statutory forms.

## Not ported
- Items tied to one company's apps (induction quiz levels, letter desk ticks, KYC link at onboarding, hiring-pipeline
  bridge, biometric auto-fill, chart code lookup).

## Later
- Letters (offer / appointment accepted), Induction and KYC can tick items once those modules are ported.
