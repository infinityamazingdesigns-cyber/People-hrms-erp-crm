# Feedback & conduct — porting notes

## Ported
- Feedback notes (Appreciation / Needs improvement with an agreed action / Concern) by anyone above the person on the
  org chart (any depth), HR or a "see all" role — never about oneself. An appreciation can be shared on the
  announcement board. Notes are taken off with a reason, never deleted; the person sees the notes about them.
- Monthly check-ins (On track / Needs support / Concern, a note unless on track) by the reporting manager, one a
  month; a list of direct reports still due.
- Conduct cases: category, severity, the day, what happened, what is expected, up to 5 code-of-conduct clauses kept
  as they were. The step follows the severity and what is on file: minor matters need a recorded note first; a
  repeat goes one step above the highest open case (minor only counts recent cases); memo → warning → final warning;
  a show-cause notice for serious matters; a safety matter is at least serious (setting).
- HR review by someone other than the raiser and never about themselves (may change severity, step and wording, send
  back to the raiser, or not issue) → approval for serious matters (setting) → issued; the person acknowledges and
  replies (late replies kept and marked; HR may record a paper reply) → HR closes with the outcome. A warning / final
  warning can be appealed once within the days set; a judge role (not the raiser) upholds (case withdrawn, kept) or
  dismisses. Cases lapse after the months set, counted from the latest case issued.
- Letter (print / save as PDF) with the organisation's name and address, wording editable in settings; the person
  is emailed that a letter is waiting (Email on), never its text. Evidence (photos, PDF; 10 MB, 12 per case) in the
  database, never shown to the person the case is about; every look audited. History per case (dc_log).
- Code of conduct typed by HR (clauses switched off, never deleted); every login accepts each version; the letter
  adds the date the person accepted it. `dc_` audit lines are hash-chained.

## Became settings
- Roles for HR review, approval, appeals and see-all; which severities need approval; safety rule; reply hours,
  appeal days, repeat months, lapse months; signatory; letter wording.

## Not ported (later)
- Suspension pending inquiry (subsistence pay is a country rule — rule pack), hearings and witness statements, POSH /
  internal committee complaints, PIP, voice replies, upward feedback, star of the month, suggestions from attendance,
  reminders, dashboard, letters in other languages, holds on probation confirmation and increments.
